Important: "total processed tokens" repeatedly counts the conversation context
sent with each model response. That is why the number is much larger than the
visible text. Cached input is included in the recorded total, but is treated
differently from uncached input by the usage system.

The account meter showed 35% used in the five-hour window when checked. A
five-hour allowance is not a fixed raw-token bucket, and the meter is shared
across qualifying activity in that window. Therefore, 35% cannot be converted
into one exact raw-token allowance or attributed solely to this chat from the
meter alone. The per-step token counts below are exact session-log counts; their
percentages use 9,287,308 as the 100% denominator requested.
